FAQ

How Much Does a Website Cost? +
Every project is uniquely tailored to your needs. A basic, high-end landing page starts at €1,290. More complex sites with CMS and multi-language support range from €2,000 to €5,000+.
What Do I Need to Get Started? +
I just need a rough idea of your goals and brand identity. We will refine everything together during our initial strategy call.
How Long Does a Project Take? +
Typically, a project takes between 2 to 6 weeks, depending on the complexity and how quickly we can iterate on feedback.
What If I Don't Like the Design? +
The design process is collaborative. We start with concepts and mood boards to ensure we are aligned before any deep coding begins, ensuring you love the final result.
Can I Update My Content Later? +
Yes, if you opt for the Content Management (CMS) add-on, you will be able to easily update text, images, and projects on your own without touching any code.
Why Are There No Monthly Costs? +
I build your site on modern, serverless architecture that often falls entirely into free tiers. You own the code completely. (Domain costs still apply).
Do You Offer Maintenance or Support After Launch? +
Yes, I offer optional retainer packages for ongoing support, feature additions, and performance monitoring.
